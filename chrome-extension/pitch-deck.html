SIMPLE BY DESIGN

From the listing address to a checked property match.

1

Open a home

Browse a supported Domain or realestate.com.au property listing.

2

Check address

Review the detected address or enter it yourself.

3

Verify match

Proceed only when one clear address match is found.

4

See available facts

Load the matched property's available Microburbs information.

5

Compare your way

Use the context alongside the listing; decide for yourself.

Trust by design: no property facts load for a non-match. The prototype uses the address for lookup and does not collect listing descriptions or photos. Coverage and data availability vary.
